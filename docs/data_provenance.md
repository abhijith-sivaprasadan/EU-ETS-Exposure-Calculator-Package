# Data and assumption provenance

The bundled workbooks and CSV files are synthetic demonstrations. They are not regulatory submissions and do not contain verified facility activity data.

Emission factors, gas conversion, free allocation, and EUA scenarios are editable assumptions. The default workbook labels them as typical or placeholder values; they must be replaced with values approved for the applicable installation, reporting year, monitoring plan, fuel basis, and jurisdiction.

For every operational use, record:

- source organisation and exact publication or supplier statement;
- factor, unit, heating-value basis, geography, and reporting year;
- access date and any transformation;
- selected EUA price source and timestamp;
- workbook version and code commit used to produce the output.

Scope 1 exposure is the EU ETS planning boundary used here. Scope 2 and optional Scope 3 values are contextual proxies and must not be presented as surrender obligations.
